# TNSURB Sub-Inspector (SI) — Complete Syllabus Documentation

Official curriculum roadmap for the TNSURB SI examination, organized systematically by subjects and topics.

---

## 📚 Subject Categories Overview

1. **Numerical Analysis (கணிதப் பகுப்பாய்வு)** — 20 Topics
   - Number System, Simplification, Percentage, Profit & Loss, Simple Interest, Compound Interest, Ratio & Proportion, Average, Problems on Ages, Time & Work, Pipes & Cisterns, Time Speed Distance, Trains, Boats & Streams, LCM & HCF, Number Series, Data Interpretation, Mixture & Alligation, Partnership, Probability.

2. **Logical Analysis (தர்க்க பகுப்பாய்வு)** — 15 Topics
   - Coding & Decoding, Blood Relations, Direction Sense, Ranking & Order, Alphabet Test, Analogy, Classification, Number Series, Alphabet Series, Seating Arrangement, Puzzles, Syllogism, Statement & Conclusion, Cause & Effect, Decision Making.

3. **Mental Ability (மனத்திறன்)** — 9 Topics
   - Inductive Reasoning, Deductive Reasoning, Non-verbal Reasoning, Mirror Images, Water Images, Paper Folding, Figure Series, Figure Completion, Pattern Recognition.

4. **Information Handling (தகவல் கையாளுதல் திறன்)** — 6 Topics
   - Table Analysis, Graph Analysis, Data Interpretation, Information Comparison, Inference Based Questions, Data Sufficiency.

5. **Communication Skills (தொடர்புத் திறன்)** — 5 Topics
   - Tamil Language, English Language, Vocabulary, Comprehension, Sentence Correction.

6. **General Knowledge (பொது அறிவு)** — 17 Topics
   - Indian History, Tamil Nadu History, World History, Indian Geography, Tamil Nadu Geography, Indian Polity, Indian Economics, General Science, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Environment, Science & Technology, Art & Culture, Sports, Awards, Current Affairs.

7. **Tamil Eligibility (தமிழ் தகுதித் தேர்வு)** — 5 Topics
   - Grammar (இலக்கணம்), Literature (இலக்கியம்), Thirukkural (திருக்குறள்), Tamil Scholars (தமிழ் அறிஞர்கள்), Language Skills (மொழித்திறன்).

---

## 🎯 The 6-Level Topic Standard

Every topic in the curriculum is bound to a strict 6-level progression:

| Level | Difficulty Standard | Target Question Standard | Question Count |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1** | Easy | Basic concepts, fundamental definitions, and core rules | 25–30 Questions |
| **Level 2** | Easy to Medium | Direct calculations and single-step applications | 25–30 Questions |
| **Level 3** | Medium | Application-based questions and standard exam patterns | 25–30 Questions |
| **Level 4** | Hard | Multi-step calculations and intermediate problem-solving | 25–30 Questions |
| **Level 5** | Difficult | Complex calculations, multiple concepts, and tricky traps | 25–30 Questions |
| **Level 6** | Genius / Advanced | Advanced TNUSRB SI exam-style problems & speed math | 25–30 Questions |
