# Question Quality & Management Standards

Guidelines for authoring, verifying, and managing practice questions for TNUSRB SI preparation.

---

## 💎 Quality Requirements

1. **Language:**
   - Question text must be written in grammatically sound, natural Tamil.
   - All four option choices must be in Tamil.
   - English technical terms must be enclosed in parentheses where helpful (e.g., `அடக்க விலை (Cost Price - CP)`).
   - Explanations must provide complete, step-by-step mathematical or logical solutions in Tamil.

2. **No Duplication:**
   - 0% repetition within the same topic across all six levels.
   - 0% repetition across different topics.
   - Changing only numeric values from an earlier level is strictly disallowed; every problem must feature distinct concepts or problem setups.

3. **Difficulty Progression:**
   - Level 1: Conceptual clarity & rules.
   - Level 2: Direct formula applications.
   - Level 3: Real-world word problems.
   - Level 4: Multi-step problems combining 2+ steps.
   - Level 5: Tricky wording and deceptive options.
   - Level 6: Advanced speed-math problems matching top TNUSRB SI standards.

4. **Authenticity:**
   - Must follow authentic TNUSRB SI patterns and difficulty.
   - Do **not** falsely label synthetic questions as official previous-year papers.
